Since its foundation, IMB has pioneered the manufacture of concrete extruders and slipform pavers in Latin America. We are the development pillar of solutions for the largest contractors and a trusted partner for entrepreneurs.

Our Story

Precision engineering, unwavering commitment.

Operating since 1992 in the metallurgy sector and in the manufacture of construction equipment, IMB has become Brazil's largest manufacturer of concrete pavers and concrete-profile extruders. Based in a modern and versatile 16,000 m² manufacturing plant in Campina Grande do Sul, in the state of Paraná, IMB relies on a technical team focused on developing and improving its product lines, always seeking to offer customers the best solutions for cast-in-place concrete profiles.

Focused on meeting and exceeding its customers' needs, IMB invests in the training of its employees, providing a better experience and closer commercial relationships.
